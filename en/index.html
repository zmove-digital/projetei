Why the concept comes first

Everything you find out before spending a single real

The concept is the only moment where changing something costs zero. After the final images, every change means rework — here it is a click away.

Every material, priced

You receive the input list item by item: brick, tile, wire, pipe, finish. Buy with researched prices, in the exact quantities.

Every space, from inside

Extremely realistic interior renders: see the living room, kitchen and suite as they will really be — before they exist.

Every centimeter, under control

Measurements validated with you, from the window span to the shower niche. Nothing "approximate" to discover later.

Your real furniture, in the design

The king bed, the 2.4 m sofa, the table for eight — placed to scale to prove they fit and leave room to move.

The sun, hour by hour, in every room

Endless tested combinations

Switch window model and color, paint, flooring and roof as many times as you like. Decide by seeing — not by imagining.
