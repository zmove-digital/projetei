Privacy Policy

How projetei.com collects, uses and protects your personal data. Last updated: October 23, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how projetei.com handles the personal data of anyone who visits the website, gets in touch or hires our services. By using the website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

projetei.com is an architecture and construction studio based in Porto Alegre, Brazil, at Av. Praia de Belas, 1212, sala 424, Porto Alegre/RS, CEP 90.110-000, CNPJ 64.197.991/0001-27. The person responsible for processing personal data is architect Filipe Krebs (CAU/RS A1817973). For privacy matters, contact us at contato@projetei.com.

2. What personal data we collect

  • Data you provide: name, email, phone/WhatsApp, city and state, information about the property and the project, messages and files sent.
  • Service records: conversation history via WhatsApp, email and forms.
  • Browsing data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, referral source and interactions, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Contracting data: when you hire a service, data required to issue documents, such as tax ID (CPF/CNPJ) and address.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive data (for example, racial origin, religious beliefs or health data), and we ask that you do not send it through our channels.

3. How we use the data

  • Respond to requests, questions and quote requests;
  • Prepare proposals, 3D concepts and technical projects;
  • Deliver the contracted services and maintain the relationship;
  • Send updates about your project;
  • Improve the website, content and browsing experience;
  • Measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns;
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations and exercise rights in legal proceedings.

4. Legal bases (LGPD)

We process personal data based on: (i) consent; (ii) performance of a contract or preliminary procedures; (iii) compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation; (iv) legitimate interest, always respecting your rights; and (v) regular exercise of rights.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with:

  • Hosting, email and cloud storage providers;
  • Analytics and advertising platforms (Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta/Facebook);
  • WhatsApp/Meta, when you choose to contact us through that channel;
  • Public authorities, when required by law.

These partners process the data under their own policies and only for the purposes described above.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and pixels to remember preferences, measure audience and evaluate campaigns. The website uses tools from Google (Analytics and Ads), Meta (Pixel) and Ahrefs, among others. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; this may affect some features. To opt out of ad personalization, use Google's and Meta's ad settings.

7. International transfers

Some providers (such as Google and Meta) may store data on servers outside Brazil. In such cases, we seek to ensure the transfer occurs with appropriate safeguards under the LGPD.

8. How long we keep data

We keep data for as long as necessary for the purposes above and for applicable legal periods (for example, tax, civil and litigation defense). After that, the data is deleted or anonymized.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (Article 18), you may request: confirmation that processing exists; access to the data; correction of incomplete or outdated data; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; portability; deletion of data processed with consent; information about sharing; and withdrawal of consent.

To exercise your rights, write to contato@projetei.com. We will respond within a reasonable period and may ask for information to confirm your identity.

10. Security

We adopt technical and administrative measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No system is entirely immune to incidents; if a relevant incident occurs, we will act in accordance with the LGPD.

11. Links to third-party sites

The website may contain links to external pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and recommend reading their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. The current version is always the one published on this page, with the update date shown below.
